About Us

Science by Sketch was founded on a simple idea: science can be easier to understand when we make it visual, creative, and fun.

We collaborate with Bergen County Academies' student publications, including Academy Chronicle, to share our initiatives with the broader school community. But our work extends beyond BCA. We partner with elementary and middle schools to introduce students to scientific concepts through interactive lessons, cartoons, drawings, and visual stories.

For many students, creative tools such as mnemonics, illustrations, and visual explanations can make complex ideas easier to understand and remember. We believe art is a language everyone can connect with, and that combining art and science can make STEM more approachable. By helping students build a strong foundation in science from an early age, we hope to spark curiosity and inspire them to explore STEM in the future.

We also organize fundraisers and bake sales to support organizations dedicated to STEM education and research. Rather than raising money for ourselves, we use these events to contribute to organizations working to expand access to STEM and advance scientific discovery.
