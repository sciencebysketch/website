Our Mission

Science by Sketch makes STEM easier to understand by turning science into cartoons, drawings, and visual stories. We want to make learning science more fun, creative, and memorable while inspiring students to explore STEM from an early age.

Drawn Conclusions

Our newsletter, Drawn Conclusions, brings Science by Sketch comics, articles, and ideas straight to your inbox. Everything is student-made, science-focused, and simple. There's no clutter, no AI-generated slop, just things worth reading.
